How It Works

Renting a book from Bibliophile Vibes is as easy as it gets.

01

Pick

Browse the collection and choose a book that calls to you.

02

Request

Send a rental request through WhatsApp. We'll confirm quickly.

03

Collect

Pick up the book or arrange local delivery.

04

Read

Enjoy the book during your rental period. Read it slowly.

05

Return

Return the book before the due date and get your deposit back.

Guidelines

Rental Rules

Simple, fair, and designed to keep the books moving.

📅

Rental Duration

Books are rented for 7, 14, or 30 days depending on the title. The duration is shown on each book's page.

🔒

Security Deposit

A refundable security deposit is required and returned when the book is returned in the same condition.

⏰

Late Returns

Late returns may incur a small daily charge. Please let us know in advance if you need more time — we'll try to accommodate.

📖

Book Condition

Please treat the books with care. Significant damage beyond normal wear may be deducted from the security deposit.

🔁

Renewals

You can renew your rental if nobody else has reserved the book. Simply message us before the due date.

❓

Lost Books

In the unfortunate case of a lost book, the replacement cost will be discussed fairly. We understand accidents happen.

Our Story

A small library
with a big heart.

Bibliophile Vibes started the way many good things do — with a pile of beloved books, a genuine love of reading, and a simple idea: what if the neighbourhood could share these stories?

We're not a big commercial bookstore. We're a small, home-based library run by readers, for readers. Every book in our collection has been chosen because it's worth your time. We believe great stories shouldn't be expensive or hard to find.

Right now we're just getting started — a handful of carefully chosen titles, growing slowly. And that's exactly what we love about it. Small, personal, and entirely yours to discover.
